How I Work

What to Expect

Not a template. A discipline built across 8 years of embedded work in government, enterprise, and banking. Every engagement follows the same rigour.

01
Stakeholder meetings & notes
Prepared guides, structured sessions. Decisions documented within 24 hours. Nothing falls through the cracks.
02
UX/UI audit of the feature
Heuristic evaluation before Figma. Prevents redesigns that recreate the same structural problems in a new visual style.
03
Review analytics
Drop-off, funnel data, error logs alongside research. Analytics tells where — research tells why.
04
Note findings, update PRD
Insights into one source of truth. PRD updated with user evidence — teams build from evidence, not assumptions.
05
Conduct research
Contextual inquiry, moderated testing, stakeholder interviews — always real users. Prioritised insight doc, not a 60-slide deck.
06
Read forums, blogs & trends
Gov UX borrows from healthcare. Enterprise dashboards borrow from fintech. Context keeps solutions grounded.
07
Ideate
HMW framing, opportunity mapping, sketches grounded in specific user needs. Silent ideation first.
08
Brainstorm ideas
15–20 concepts before evaluating any. The PROTECT split IA started as an idea the PM initially dismissed.
09
Sketching
Paper first. Rough sketches get honest feedback on structure — not colour.
10
Digitised into UI
Component library from day one. Every state, edge case, responsive behaviour annotated. My designs survive handoff.
